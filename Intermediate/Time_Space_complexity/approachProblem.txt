==============================
HOW TO APPROACH A PROBLEM
==============================

1. Read the question and constraints carefully.

2. Formulate an idea and logic.

3. Verify the correctness of the logic.

4. Mentally develop a pseudocode (or write a rough pseudocode).

5. Determine the Time Complexity (TC) based on the pseudocode.

6. Assess whether the TC is feasible and won't result in a TLE.
   - As a rule of thumb, the worst-case operations should generally be
     around 10^7 to 10^8.

7. If the TC is not feasible, re-evaluate and optimize the idea/logic.

8. Otherwise, proceed to code the solution.

9. Dry-run the solution with sample and edge test cases.

10. Submit the solution.
